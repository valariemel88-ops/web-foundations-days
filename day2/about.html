About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple app for capturing and organising your notes. It helps you quickly save ideas, reminders, and important information in one place.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open QuickNotes and go to the note form.
  2. Type your note into the text box.
  3. Click the Add Note button to save your note.

Features

  • Quickly create notes.
  • Keep your notes organised.
  • Simple and easy-to-use interface.
  • Access your notes from the QuickNotes page.

Keyboard shortcuts
